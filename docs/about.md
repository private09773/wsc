## Our Story
The Website Markup Language Specification Consortium (WSC) 
was founded on August 30, 2026 with a simple but ambitious goal: 
to create a clean, strict (or forgiving), and accessible markup language for the web.

---

**The Beginning**
WSC began as a solo project — one developer, one vision, and one question: 
"What if web standards were simple again?"

Dissatisfied with the complexity and loose syntax of modern web technologies, 
the founder set out to build something different. Something that 
combined the rigor of XML with the familiarity of HTML. 
Something that prioritized clarity, structure, 
and semantic correctness over experimental features.

That vision became WML (Website Markup Language) —
a strict, XML-based markup language built upon
the foundation of HTML 4.01 Strict.

---

**Why WSC Exists**

- Maintain and evolve the WML specification
- Provide a clear, stable alternative to modern web standards
- Keep the web accessible — not just for professionals, but for everyone
- Preserve the principles of SGML and XML as the underlying grammar for structured documents

WSC is not affiliated with, 
derived from, or governed by 
the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). 
We are an independent body, 
built by a hobbyist for hobbyists, 
developers, and anyone who 
values clean syntax and semantic structure.

--- 

**The WML Family**

WML is just the beginning. 
The WML Family of specifications
will continue to grow — always guided by the same philosophy:

> "Non opus est ut magnum sit."
> (It does not need to be great. It just needs to work.)

---

**Today**

WSC operates as a small, 
independent standards body. 
All specifications are publicly 
available and freely implementable. 
There are no corporate sponsors, 
no membership fees, and no vendor 
lock-in — just a commitment to the 
open web and the belief that web 
standards should be accessible 
to everyone.

---

**Join Us**

WSC is always looking for contributors,
testers, and enthusiasts who share our 
vision. Whether you're a seasoned developer 
or just getting started, there's a place for 
you in the WSC community.
